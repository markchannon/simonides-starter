# Simonides starter

A blank second brain for Claude, ready to make your own. One private folder of plain-text notes that Claude reads before it answers and writes to when you say "remember this", kept safe on GitHub, tidied every morning by a scheduled job, with one page that tells you what was done and what needs you.

This repository holds no one's personal data. It is the empty structure: folders, rules, skills, the daily tidy prompt, the daily page with sample data, a checker and a sync script. You fill it with your own world.

## Start
1. Click **Use this template**, then **Create a new repository**. Name it `simonides`, choose **Private**, and create it. Keep your copy private: it will hold your life and work.
2. Open **Simonides: The Complete Guide** at "Start here", as a page (https://claude.ai/artifact/V17eykRieJ1eWN8TQVRn2T) or in this repository as [_BUILD-GUIDE.md](_BUILD-GUIDE.md). It has the one message that puts your copy on your computer and makes it yours.
3. Then follow the recipes in order, starting each one's chat with the guide's kickoff prompt.

You do not need to write code. You talk to Claude; Claude does the technical parts. Every step in the guide has the exact words to send.

Simonides of Ceos, the Greek poet credited with inventing the memory palace, the oldest method there is for remembering anything.
