# Start here

You have your own copy of the Simonides starter kit. Everything you need is in the guide, _BUILD-GUIDE.md.

**1. If you have not yet:** follow "Get the starter kit" in the guide's Start here. Its message clones your copy to ~/Simonides on your computer and personalises it (your GitHub username, your worlds, your time zone, the client folders kept off every page).

**2. Then the recipes, in order,** each in its own chat, started with the guide's kickoff prompt. The kit already holds the files the recipes use; Claude checks them and makes them yours.

**3. A few blanks are filled later,** by the recipe that makes the thing they point to:
- the Installed column in `_SKILLS.md`: when you install each skill (recipes 3 and 6)
- the one-minute version in `_EXPLAINER.md`: recipe 5
- the page link in `projects/artifacts-index.md` and `systems/simonides-daily-dashboard/tidy-task-prompt.md`: recipe 8
